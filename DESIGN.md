## Theme Modes

This system ships with two themes that share the same typography, shape language, spacing scale and component anatomy. Only color, elevation treatment and a few component states change.

- **System** is the startup default; light is used when the OS reports light mode. Tokens live under `colors`.
- **Dark** tokens live under `colors-dark` and use the exact same token names, so switching theme means swapping the map, not renaming anything.
- Allow users to choose light, dark, or system in app settings. Save the choice across launches; choosing system resumes following device appearance, including changes while the app is running.
- Never mix tokens from both maps on one screen.

## Brand & Style

The brand personality embodies freshness, authentic botanical vitality, uncompromised purity, and sustainable craftsmanship rooted in Vietnamese agriculture. It serves health-conscious consumers, modern urban families, and eco-aware buyers looking for traceable, organic farm-to-table products. The tone is authentic, vital and reassuring: it confirms origin, organic certification and freshness while keeping navigation effortless.

**Light mood:** modern eco-minimalism. Generous pure white canvas, botanical emerald accents and crisp sprout greens. Organic shapes, breathable compositions and tactile clarity, evoking morning dew on highland tea leaves and freshly harvested greens.

**Dark mood:** a high-performance, OLED-optimized, bioluminescent night-market feel, evoking terraced fields and greenhouse canopies at twilight. It avoids muddy "organic" tropes. Depth comes from stacked charcoal surfaces, subtle emerald luminescence and pristine typography.

## Typography

**Be Vietnam Pro** is the only typeface, across display, editorial, UI labels and data. It has native, well-balanced diacritic support for Vietnamese, prevents glyph clipping, and stays readable on compact screens.

- Titles and headlines use SemiBold (600); display uses Bold (700) for structural grounding.
- Body text uses Regular (400) with generous line height (`22px` for 14px text).
- Key data (agricultural weights, origin, certifications, prices) uses Medium (500), SemiBold (600) or Bold (700).
- **Dark mode rule:** never use weights below 400 on dark surfaces.
- Dense numeric values (VND prices, batch weights) should use tabular figures when available.
- `display-xl` (44px) is intended mainly for dark-mode hero and desktop contexts; `display-lg` (36px) is the standard display size.

Typography tokens are available as NativeWind utilities. Combine `font-vietnam` with a `text-*` token; the token supplies its size, line height, tracking and weight:

```tsx
<Text className="font-vietnam text-headline-md text-ink">
  Sản phẩm hữu cơ
</Text>

<Text className="font-vietnam text-body-md text-muted">
  Rau củ tươi, có nguồn gốc rõ ràng.
</Text>
```

The Be Vietnam Pro Regular, Medium, SemiBold and Bold font files are stored in `assets/fonts` and loaded in the root Expo Router layout before the app is shown.

## Layout & Spacing

Mobile-first, thumb-reach commerce layout.

- **Mobile (<640px):** 4-column fluid grid. Margins `16px` (`margin-mobile`), up to `20px` on wider phones; gutter `12-16px`.
- **Tablet (640-1024px):** 8-column grid, `1.5rem` margins, `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column grid, max width `1280px`, `2rem` margins, `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Rhythm:** 8pt system with 4pt half-steps for compact metadata and badges. `space-xs` (4px) micro-alignment, `space-sm` (8px) icon-text pairs, `space-md` (16px) card padding, `space-lg` (24px) section clusters, `space-xl` (32px) and `space-2xl` (40px) for major market sections.
- **Sticky zones:** bottom navigation and the "Thêm vào giỏ" (Add to cart) sheet dock with `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` padding. In dark mode they sit over a semi-translucent `#121614` scrim.

## Elevation & Depth

No heavy, artificial drop shadows in either theme. Depth comes from tonal layering.

### Light

- **Card depth:** `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(0, 107, 53, 0.05)` plus micro-border `1px solid #F0F4EC`.
- **Overlays and modals:** floating action bars and bottom sheets use `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` with backdrop blur and a hairline green-tint border.

### Dark

- **Tonal stacking:** `#121614` base, `#1A201C` card, `#242C26` floating sheet or action.
- **Emerald border glow:** `border: 1px solid rgba(0, 230, 118, 0.15)` with `box-shadow: 0 4px 24px -2px rgba(0, 107, 53, 0.25)`.
- **Active and focus glow:** border intensifies to `rgba(56, 193, 114, 0.45)` with bloom `0 0 16px rgba(0, 230, 118, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

A soft, curvilinear shape language echoing leaves and seed pods.

- **Base radius `0.5rem` (8px):** buttons, inputs, nested image previews, interactive list cells.
- **Large radius `1rem` (16px):** cards, product showcase containers, bottom sheets, dialogs.
- **Full pill `9999px`:** category indicators, origin pills, status tags, certification badges, count selectors, pill CTAs.
- Checkbox radius: `4px` (light) / `6px` (dark).

- **Certification chips (VietGAP, Organic, Traceable)**
  - Light: pill, background `#EBF6E9`, text `#006B35`, optional 12px leaf glyph.
  - Dark: pill, background `#006B35` at 85% opacity, text `#F0FDF4`, border `1px solid rgba(0, 230, 118, 0.3)`.
- **Filter chips**
  - Light: inactive is `#FFFFFF` with `#E5E7EB` border; active is `#58A500` fill with white text.
  - Dark: inactive is `#242C26` with `#94A3B8` text; active is `#38C172` fill with `#121614` bold text.

### Product & Story Cards

- Light: `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #F0F4EC`, `16px` inner padding. Photography at the top edge with an 8px inner radius, clear origin labels and fresh green price badges.
- Dark: `#1A201C` background, `rounded-2xl`, signature `1px solid rgba(0, 230, 118, 0.12)` border. Image containers use 1:1 or 4:3 ratios with inset vignette masks so produce photography dissolves into the surface.

### Input Fields

- Shared: 48px height, labels above the field in Medium (500).
- Light: white background, `1px solid #D1D5DB`. Focus: border `#006B35` plus aura `rgba(0, 107, 53, 0.12)`. Labels `#000000`.
- Dark: background `#1A201C`, border `#2D3730`, placeholder `#94A3B8`. Focus: border `#38C172` plus `0 0 0 3px rgba(56, 193, 114, 0.15)`. Search and checkout fields follow this style.

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls

- Light: rounded-square (4px) with `#58A500` fill and white check; radios have a concentric Sprout Green inner pip.
- Dark: rounded-square (6px) or circular frames with `#2D3730` borders; selected fill `#38C172` with a dark `#121614` check mark.

### Bottom Navigation Bar

- Fixed at the screen bottom, clean line icons, labels in `label-sm`.
- Light: pure white surface; active destination in Brand Green `#006B35` with a subtle upper sprout indicator pip.
- Dark: translucent `#121614` surface; active destination in `#38C172` with the same indicator pip.

### Produce Origin Tracker & Freshness Gauge (domain-specific)

A segmented progress bar showing harvest time, packing status and regional origin (e.g. Đà Lạt, Mộc Châu). Active segments use a linear gradient from `#006B35` to `#00E676`. In light mode, place the gauge on a `#EBF6E9` track; in dark mode, on a `#2D3730` track.
